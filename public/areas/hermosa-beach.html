Compact beach town of Strand homes, walk streets and hillside view lots

Hermosa Beach is a small, densely built city between Manhattan Beach and Redondo Beach, organized around Pier Avenue, Pier Plaza and the Strand. Older cottages, side-by-side condominiums and Strand frontage come up often in trust and estate work here, where land value, views and distance from the sand drive most of the discussion.

Homes in Hermosa Beach

A few 1920s cottages survive, but much of the Sand Section has been rebuilt with multi-level modern homes and two-unit condominiums on narrow lots. Walk streets west of Hermosa Avenue trade car frontage for landscaped pedestrian paths, and many of the sloped ones face the ocean. From the Hermosa Valley Greenbelt, the Valley Section climbs hilly streets toward Pacific Coast Highway, and the Hill Section on the far side of the highway mixes houses, townhomes and view condominiums.

What moves value in Hermosa Beach

Position relative to the sand sets the tiers, from Strand frontage to walk streets, the rest of the Sand Section, the Valley Section and the Hill Section, where views can offset distance. Small lots and scarce parking give garage spaces, roof-deck views and rebuild potential real weight, and blocks nearest Pier Plaza trade walkability against nightlife noise.

Appraisal considerations in Hermosa Beach

Older Sand Section cottages often sell for their site value, so land sales and a highest-and-best-use analysis sit alongside improved comparables. Two-unit condominiums are matched with similar projects, noting shared walls and any maintenance agreement, and for walk-street and Strand homes, paired sales from the same block band isolate the frontage premium.

Mom's 1920s Sand Section cottage is dated; will the appraisal treat it as a teardown?

Only where the market supports it. We compare the cottage with sales of similar older homes and with purchases where buyers paid mainly for the site, and if the land alone outweighs the property as it stands, the report says so and shows the supporting sales.
