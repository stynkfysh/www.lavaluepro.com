Homes in Leimert Park

The residential streets are known for Spanish Colonial Revival houses from the neighborhood's early decades, set along tree-lined blocks. Leimert Park Village, the commercial core around Leimert Plaza Park, Degnan Boulevard and 43rd Street, anchors the original plan. Published boundaries differ, with some maps extending the neighborhood north to Exposition Boulevard and east to Arlington Avenue, so a subject's exact location matters when comparables are chosen. Because so many houses date from the same era, differences in remodeling, original detail and lot position usually matter more than age.

What moves value in Leimert Park

Demand draws on the neighborhood's period architecture, the cultural life of Leimert Park Village and the K Line, whose Leimert Park station opened in October 2022 and connects north to the E Line. Buyers commonly compare homes here with Jefferson Park, Baldwin Vista and View Park, and street setting, condition and the degree of original detail separate otherwise similar houses.

Local reference points: Leimert Plaza Park, designed by the Olmsted Brothers and listed as a Historic-Cultural Monument, the Vision Theatre, the shops of Degnan Boulevard and 43rd Street, Crenshaw Square and the K Line's Leimert Park station are neighborhood landmarks.

Appraisal considerations in Leimert Park

Individual Historic-Cultural Monuments exist here, including a single-family house on Olmstead Avenue, so designation status should be confirmed for any older house before redevelopment is assumed. For effective dates before the K Line opened in October 2022, comparables should reflect conditions along Crenshaw Boulevard at that time rather than today's transit access. Interior photos matter a great deal in a desktop review here, since original tile, woodwork and plaster detail can separate a restored house from one remodeled without regard to its period.

Did the K Line opening change home values near the Leimert Park station?

We do not assume a fixed effect. We compare sales near the station with similar homes farther away, before and after the line opened in October 2022, and apply only the difference the data supports for the effective date in question.
