Homes in Silver Lake

Ivanhoe, north of the reservoir, was laid out in 1887 with many streets named after figures from British literature, and Moreno Highlands around the water was developed in the 1920s and 1930s. Modernist architects including Richard Neutra, Rudolph Schindler, John Lautner, Gregory Ain, and Raphael Soriano designed homes and studios here. Public staircases climb many of the steeper streets, and newer small-lot homes and some lofts sit among older bungalows and apartment buildings.

What moves value in Silver Lake

Reservoir and skyline views, walkability to Sunset Boulevard, and architectural significance drive the strongest prices, while homes on busier streets or near the freeways trade lower. Design-minded buyers often pay for architectural character and original detail. Small-lot homes and condominiums form a separate submarket from detached houses on standard lots.

Appraisal considerations in Silver Lake

View premiums vary widely; a direct reservoir view, a partial view, and a downslope house below street level each call for different comparables. Small-lot homes are owned fee-simple but typically share access driveways and maintenance agreements, so they are compared with other small-lot sales rather than with conventional houses. Hillside lots get the usual review of slope, access, and parking.

Should a Silver Lake small-lot home be compared with condos or with houses?

With neither, at first. Small-lot homes are detached and owned fee-simple, yet they sit on shared driveways and usually have compact yards, so the most reliable evidence is other small-lot sales nearby. We bring in houses or condominiums only as secondary support and explain the adjustments.
