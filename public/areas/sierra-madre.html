Foothill village of cottages, bungalows, and canyon cabins below Mount Wilson

Sierra Madre is a foothill city of about three square miles between Pasadena and Arcadia, with Sierra Madre Boulevard as its main street. Executors and family members here usually ask about long-held houses passing through a trust, canyon cabins with unusual access, and whether the 2025 Eaton Fire should influence a value even where the house came through untouched.

Homes in Sierra Madre

Older cottages and Craftsman bungalows line the grid near downtown, while later ranch and custom homes climb toward the foothills. In Lower and Upper Sierra Madre Canyon, small cabins and bungalows sit along narrow, winding roads, often on irregular lots with little parking. Apartments, duplexes, and a few condominium buildings add attached housing, but detached houses dominate.

What moves value in Sierra Madre

Buyers come for the small-town scale, walkable downtown, and trail access, and inventory is thin because the city is almost fully built out. Steep driveways, street parking, and brush-clearance duties narrow the pool for some canyon and hillside homes, so comparables are often drawn from nearby foothill areas of Pasadena and Arcadia.

Appraisal considerations in Sierra Madre

The Eaton Fire destroyed a small number of structures along Sierra Madre's northern edge and burned the wilderness above it, so retrospective work separates pre-fire and post-fire market conditions. For later effective dates, we note burn-scar debris-flow exposure and insurance availability for canyon and hillside parcels, and canyon lots call for close reading of lot lines, access easements, and older unpermitted additions.

Our Sierra Madre home survived the Eaton Fire untouched; does the fire still affect its value?

It may, mainly through the market rather than the house itself. For dates after January 2025, we test whether foothill and canyon sales show changes in demand, insurance availability, or marketing time, and compare them with sales farther from the burn area before drawing any conclusion.
