Manhattan Beach home and condo appraisals for estates, trusts, and taxes

Leveled-dune beach city of rebuilt lots, walk streets and eastside tracts

Manhattan Beach runs along about two miles of shoreline between El Segundo and Hermosa Beach, with the Sand, Hill and Tree sections west of Sepulveda Boulevard and a broad eastside of mid-century neighborhoods beyond it. When an executor inherits an original house here, the land usually carries most of the value, and the central question becomes how much the older structure still contributes.

Homes in Manhattan Beach

The dunes were graded away in the 1920s and 1930s, and custom homes have replaced most early cottages. The Tree Section has larger interior lots, the Hill Section has view homes on its slopes, and El Porto is known for half lots. East of Sepulveda, mid-century houses in Manhattan Heights and Liberty Village are steadily being rebuilt, the Poets Section has larger lots, and gated Manhattan Village mixes court homes, townhomes and estate homes.

What moves value in Manhattan Beach

Lot size, frontage and distance to the sand matter more than the age of the house. Ocean-view premiums depend on angle, breadth and the risk of obstruction, and east of Sepulveda, original houses draw rebuilders, with new-construction sales setting the ceiling.

Appraisal considerations in Manhattan Beach

Because many buyers are paying for the lot, land and teardown purchases are analyzed beside improved sales to show what the existing house adds. Since 2010 new homes may not span more than two lots, which caps any assemblage premium, and in El Porto, half-lot homes and the neighboring Chevron refinery are handled with comparables from the same blocks.

Dad's original 1950s Tree Section house sits on a large lot; do new-build sales set its value?

Not directly. New homes show what finished product brings, but an original house is compared with other original or lightly updated homes and with lots bought for rebuilding, and the gap between those groups shows what the existing improvements contribute.
