Rancho Palos Verdes home and condo appraisals for estates, trusts, and taxes

Peninsula city of ocean-view hillsides, coastal tracts and active landslide ground

Rancho Palos Verdes covers the southern and eastern slopes of the Palos Verdes Peninsula, from Miraleste above the harbor to the ocean bluffs at Point Vicente and Portuguese Bend. A mostly single-family city incorporated in 1973, it brings estate questions centered on views, geologic hazards and the active landslide complex.

Homes in Rancho Palos Verdes

Much of the housing dates from the 1950s through the 1970s, with ranch and contemporary homes stepping down hillsides toward the water. Miraleste lots are larger but often steep, west-side tracts such as Lunada Pointe hold newer and larger homes, and townhomes concentrate in the Crest area and along Palos Verdes Drive South. Many Eastview streets carry San Pedro addresses.

What moves value in Rancho Palos Verdes

View quality and lot position drive value more than almost anything else, and homes with unobstructed ocean and Catalina outlooks sit at the top of the local range. Distance from the landslide complex became a market factor once movement accelerated in 2023 and 2024, and city monitoring still showed movement in 2026.

Appraisal considerations in Rancho Palos Verdes

The Greater Portuguese Bend–Ancient Altamira landslide complex nearly doubled in active area by 2024, bringing red and yellow tags, utility shutoffs and emergency declarations to Seaview, the Portuguese Bend Beach Club and the Portuguese Bend Community Association, and later a FEMA-funded voluntary buyout program. Values must reflect tag status, utility service and landslide-moratorium limits in force on the effective date, with sales inside the complex examined individually.

For a 2024 date of death in Portuguese Bend, how are sales chosen amid the land movement?

We start with sales and listings inside the complex close to the effective date and document each one's condition, tag status and utility service. Where market sales are scarce, the report explains the gap, weighs earlier sales adjusted for conditions known on that date, and states the limitations plainly.
