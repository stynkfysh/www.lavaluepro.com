Planned community of condominiums and townhomes on the former Hughes Aircraft site

Playa Vista is a master-planned community in the City of Los Angeles built since 2001 on the former Hughes Aircraft property, beneath the Westchester Bluffs and beside the Ballona Wetlands. Valuation requests here involve condominiums and townhomes held in trusts, date-of-separation values for homes bought new, and family transfers where association dues and community facilities must be considered.

Homes in Playa Vista

Development began with the first phase in 2001 and continued with the later Village phase, producing a housing stock made up largely of condominiums, townhomes and apartments. Buildings date from the early 2000s onward, so age differences are smaller than in older neighborhoods, but floor plans, building types and locations within the community vary considerably. Residential blocks share the community with offices, retail at Runway and the tech campuses that have made the area part of Silicon Beach.

What moves value in Playa Vista

Buyers are drawn by newer construction, parks and walkability to offices and shops, and by short drives to the beach and LAX. Values depend on product type, unit size, position relative to parks and streets, views of the bluffs or wetlands, and total association costs. Many buyers compare Playa Vista with newer product in Culver City, Marina del Rey and the South Bay.

Appraisal considerations in Playa Vista

Owners pay dues to the Playa Vista Parks and Landscape Corporation, which operates shared facilities such as The Resort and The CenterPointe Club, and condominium buildings carry their own association dues as well, so total monthly obligations belong in the analysis. Because of documented methane in the area, the City required membrane barriers, venting and alarms beneath buildings, and buyers may ask about these systems. Comparables are best drawn from the same phase and product type, since a townhome and a condominium flat of similar size can sell quite differently.

Are Playa Vista association dues considered when valuing a townhome for an estate?

Yes. Buyers weigh the combined cost of the community-wide dues and any building or tract association, along with the amenities those dues provide, so we compare the subject with sales carrying similar obligations and note any special assessments in place on the effective date.
